## 2026.11.2 · Calmer notifications
- **Grouped updates** — A burst about one project shows as one group, like “6 updates on Payments”, and pings you once.
- **Urgent rings, the rest waits** — Approvals, mentions, what is due today, incidents and calls ring. Everything else is a quiet badge. Tune it in Settings › Notifications › Smart.
- **One daily summary** — Get normal updates together in one e-mail at a time you choose, in your own time zone.
- **Mute a project or thread** — Open a group in the Notification Centre and choose Mute. It still lands there, it just never rings.
- **Report a problem** — From the account menu or Settings. A screenshot and the recent errors come along, so we can fix it faster.

## 2026.11.1 · Live everywhere
- **Changes appear as they happen** — Boards, lists and chats update the moment someone else changes them.
- **Every date keeps its history** — When a date moves, the reason stays with it.
